# Scene draft prompt v1

Read docs/WORLD.md, CHARACTERS.md and STORY.md. Draft exactly one scene for a 5–10 minute reunion sample. Every spoken line should be short, concrete and believable; narrator observes actions rather than summarizing emotions. Do not invent world rules, change ages, add new illustrated characters, lock major future plot, or resolve all relationship problems.

Output scene ID, location, time, asset IDs, line IDs, speakers and choice effects in the existing JSON schema. Choices must have observable replies and state consequences. Review emotional continuity manually after mechanical lint.
